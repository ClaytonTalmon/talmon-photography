About

Clayton Talmon de l’Armée is an American photographer whose practice is rooted in more than thirty-five years of monochrome image-making across still photography and cinema.

He produces his photographs as traditional silver-gelatin or archival pigment prints. Printing remains central to the work: tonal separation, surface and scale are treated as compositional decisions, not technical outcomes.
